Chinedu Okafor called to ask whether the family office could add to private credit. Explained that the Okafor Family Office's commitment to Private Credit Fund II is set; additional exposure comes only as the undrawn balance is called for new loans. Discussed direct lending yields versus core bonds and the floating-rate structure of the fund's loans: coupons reset with short-term rates, so income would drift lower if policy rates are cut, but there is little duration risk. Chinedu Okafor accepted the explanation and asked to hear about new loans as they reach the Investment Committee. No change to the commitment.
