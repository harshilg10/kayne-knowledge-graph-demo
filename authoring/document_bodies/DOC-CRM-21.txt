Maria Delgado called to ask how rate cuts would affect the income she draws from her accounts. Explained that the Core Fixed Income Fund's yield would drift lower gradually as maturing bonds are reinvested, rather than drop all at once, and that the firm expects fewer rate cuts than the market is pricing. Also walked her through the shorter duration target adopted in March, from 6.0 to 5.0 years: the fund is now less sensitive to swings in long-term yields, at the cost of some price upside if yields fall sharply. The change was made inside the fund and required no trades in her IRA or the trust. Maria satisfied; no changes.
