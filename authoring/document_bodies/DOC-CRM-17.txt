Diane Whitman emailed asking why the foundation's bond allocation has been steadier this year than in prior years. Replied the same day. In March the Investment Committee approved IC-2026-005, which shortened the Core Fixed Income Fund duration target from 6.0 to 5.0 years. A shorter duration target makes the fund less sensitive to moves in long-term yields, which has helped dampen swings in the foundation's bond holdings; the trade-off is less upside if yields fall sharply. The change was made inside the fund, mainly by selling longer-dated Treasuries, and required no trades in the foundation's account. Diane plans to share the explanation with her board; offered a call with the board if useful.
