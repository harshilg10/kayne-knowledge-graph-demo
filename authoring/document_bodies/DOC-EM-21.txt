For the record, a short summary of the Q3 review I presented at yesterday's Investment Committee.

- GPU capacity is still tight. Our channel work says accelerator supply remains allocated, with advanced packaging and high-bandwidth memory still the binding constraints.
- Hyperscaler capex looks intact. Our read of spending plans shows no sign of a pullback, and inference demand is becoming a larger share of the build.
- Our view that demand holds through 2027 is unchanged. The risks are the ones we flagged last November - a digestion phase, export controls and valuation - plus power availability at new sites.

On positioning, the committee kept the AI Infrastructure Fund at 8% of the Growth model, recorded in IC-2026-013 alongside the Polar Loop Cooling Systems facility. Nvidia sits at the 10% single-name cap within the fund, and Brendan Kessler plans to keep it there; the cap leaves room for broader AI infrastructure exposure across networking, memory, power and cooling. Elena Vasquez also confirmed that, after the overlap-reduction trades in late July and early August, the three flagged clients are back below the 15% look-through guideline.

I will refresh the view at the next quarterly review.
