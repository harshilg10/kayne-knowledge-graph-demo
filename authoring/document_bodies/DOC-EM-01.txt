You will have seen a new holding on the trust's second-quarter statement, so I wanted to explain it. We added about $1.51 million of the Energy Infrastructure Fund to the Hartwell Family Trust taxable account on June 23, funded by selling part of the trust's Municipal Income Fund position. The trade implemented IC-2025-014, our Investment Committee's June 18 decision to initiate the fund at a 5% weight in our growth and balanced model portfolios.

The thesis is steady cash flow rather than a bet on commodity prices. The fund owns midstream businesses - natural gas pipelines, storage and processing - that earn most of their revenue from fee-based contracts, so results depend more on the volumes moving through the system than on where natural gas prices go. Williams Companies and Kinder Morgan are two of the larger holdings and good examples of that model. The trade-off is that the position produces taxable income in place of a slice of the trust's tax-exempt income, which we judged acceptable at a 5% weight.
