Discussion:
Item 1 - Polar Loop Cooling Systems (Marcus Bell, Priya Nair)
- Marcus Bell and Priya Nair presented a $20 million senior secured facility from Private Credit Fund II to Polar Loop Cooling Systems, a manufacturer of direct-to-chip liquid cooling systems, following their August 27 meeting with CEO Henrik Larsen. Proceeds fund a second manufacturing line.
- Priya Nair: backlog is driven by rising rack density in AI data centers, where the newest GPU racks run beyond what air cooling can handle. Customers include Cobalt Ridge Data Centers, already a Private Credit Fund II borrower, so the two loans will be monitored as related exposure. Main risks are customer concentration and component supply.
- Elena Vasquez: following the overlap-reduction trades completed in early August, the three clients flagged in June are back below the 15% guideline. The capital call adds roughly one percentage point of look-through exposure for clients in Private Credit Fund II, which keeps them below 15%.

Item 2 - Quarterly technology review (Meredith Calloway)
- Meredith Calloway: GPU capacity is still tight and demand is expected to hold through 2027; hyperscaler capex plans remain intact. Nvidia sits at the 10% single-name cap in the AI Infrastructure Fund.
- The committee agreed to keep the AI Infrastructure Fund model weight at 8% in Growth models. Elena Vasquez supported holding rather than adding, given the new cap and the overlap controls now in place.

Decisions:
- IC-2026-013, approved 7-0, covering both items: (1) the $20 million senior secured facility to Polar Loop Cooling Systems, funded by Private Credit Fund II capital call #7 on September 15; (2) the AI Infrastructure Fund model weight maintained at 8%.
