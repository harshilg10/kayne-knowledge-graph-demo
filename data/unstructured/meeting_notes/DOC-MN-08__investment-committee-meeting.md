Discussion:
- Rebecca Thornton, Natalie Brooks and Wade Pruitt proposed raising the Energy Infrastructure Fund from 5% to 7% in Growth and Balanced models, funded from fixed income (memo of May 7).
- Natalie Brooks: in her view, data center power demand is now the main driver of load growth, and much of the supply being added to serve it is gas-fired generation, often on site because interconnection queues are long. She referred to her January report, "Powering AI: Data Center Electricity Demand and the Grid".
- Wade Pruitt: each new gas-fired plant needs firm gas supply, which translates into demand for natural gas pipelines and expansion projects. In his April report, "Natural Gas Midstream: The Data Center Power Trade", he sees Williams Companies and Kinder Morgan as best positioned, with fee-based contracts giving visibility on returns.
- Rebecca Thornton: power producers already in the fund, Constellation Energy and Vistra, benefit from tighter power markets, so the increase strengthens both the midstream and the power legs of the strategy.
- Elena Vasquez asked about overlap with the AI Infrastructure Fund, which also holds Constellation Energy. Rebecca Thornton: the overlap is limited to a few power and grid names and is small at the portfolio level.
- Philip Ashworth confirmed the Bellamy Foundation is exempt because its investment policy excludes fossil-fuel infrastructure.

Decisions:
- IC-2026-008: raise the Energy Infrastructure Fund from 5% to 7% in Growth and Balanced models; the Bellamy Foundation is exempt under its fossil-fuel exclusion. Approved 7-0.
