The Risk Committee reviewed the first look-through exposure report, which Elena Vasquez requested in February when she dissented on IC-2026-004.

Discussion:
- Method (Lucas Meyer): client fund positions x fund holdings x company theme exposure (high 1.0, medium 0.5, low 0.25). Positions as of June 15, aggregated across each client's accounts, private investment accounts included.
- Results: three clients exceed the 15% AI infrastructure guideline - Hartwell Family Trust 15.9%, Okafor Family Office 16.3% and Sterling-Hayes Family Office 16.5%. Next highest is Nakamura Family Partners at 12.7%; all other clients are below 13%.
- Drivers: each flagged client has a client-directed technology overweight in the Global Technology Fund that overlaps with the AI Infrastructure Fund. Nvidia is the largest single-name look-through exposure because it sits in both funds. Private Credit Fund II's loan to Cobalt Ridge Data Centers adds data center exposure for clients with private investment accounts.
- Douglas Whitfield asked whether model weights alone put anyone over the guideline. Lucas Meyer: no - clients at model weights, including Nakamura Family Partners with its Private Credit Fund II commitment, stay below 13%. The excess comes mainly from the overweights.
- Elena Vasquez said this is the concentration she raised at the February meeting and wants the committee to consider a single-name limit inside thematic funds as well as client-level remedies for the three flagged clients.
- Grace Holloway asked advisors to document client consent for the overweights, since they sit outside the models; each file should show that the client directed the position and was told about the overlap.
- Philip Ashworth noted that Elaine Hartwell asked in March for the trust's total Nvidia exposure; this report answers that request.
