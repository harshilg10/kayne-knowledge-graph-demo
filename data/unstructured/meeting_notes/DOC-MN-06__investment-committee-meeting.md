Discussion:
Item 1 - Core Fixed Income Fund duration (Stephen Albrecht)
- Stephen Albrecht proposed shortening the Core Fixed Income Fund duration target from 6.0 to 5.0 years, as set out in his March 5 memo. Rationale: the rate path is uncertain, the market may be pricing more cuts than will be delivered, and Treasury yields have been volatile. A shorter target reduces the fund's sensitivity to a further rise in long-end yields.
- Implementation is inside the fund, mainly by selling longer Treasuries; no client account trades are needed. The Municipal Income Fund is unchanged.
- Elena Vasquez supported the change as a reduction in duration risk for income-focused clients.

Item 2 - Private credit pipeline (Marcus Bell)
- Marcus Bell previewed a potential $45 million senior secured term loan to Cobalt Ridge Data Centers for Private Credit Fund II. The borrower is developing a 120 MW data center campus in West Texas.
- Due diligence is under way, led by Priya Nair. A site visit is scheduled for March 18 and an independent power assessment is pending. Formal credit approval is expected to come to the committee in April.
- Douglas Whitfield asked that the credit memo cover power availability, tenant quality and how the loan adds to clients' existing data center exposure.

Item 3 - IC-2026-004 implementation (Philip Ashworth)
- Philip Ashworth confirmed the IC-2026-004 rebalancing finished on March 6. Some clients have asked how much Nvidia they hold across funds; advisors are pointing them to the look-through review due by mid-year.
