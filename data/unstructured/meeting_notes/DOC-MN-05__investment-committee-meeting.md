Discussion:
- Meredith Calloway and Brendan Kessler presented their memo, "Proposal: Increase AI Infrastructure Allocation in Growth and Balanced Models", prepared at Douglas Whitfield's request after the January 15 meeting.
- Meredith Calloway: demand for GPU capacity is expected to continue through 2027. Hyperscaler capex plans have been raised again, and supply remains constrained by TSMC advanced packaging.
- Brendan Kessler: the opportunity extends beyond the GPU itself. Beneficiaries include Nvidia, Broadcom and TSMC, plus power and cooling names such as Vertiv. Nvidia is currently 12% of the AI Infrastructure Fund.
- Olivia Grant noted the overlap with the Global Technology Fund, where Nvidia is about 8% of the portfolio. Clients who hold both funds will see their combined Nvidia exposure rise with this change.
- Elena Vasquez opposed the increase on concentration grounds. For clients with technology overweights, the theme is already a meaningful share of the portfolio once the Global Technology Fund is counted, and the firm does not yet measure exposure on a look-through basis. She asked for a look-through exposure review by mid-year.
- Philip Ashworth confirmed the funding approach in the memo and said advisors will need talking points on both the rationale and the overlap question.
- Douglas Whitfield noted the committee's January view on GPU demand is unchanged and called the vote.

Decisions:
- IC-2026-004: raise the AI Infrastructure Fund model weight from 5% to 8% in Growth models and from 3% to 5% in Balanced models, funded from fixed income (Municipal Income Fund in taxable accounts, Core Fixed Income Fund otherwise). Approved 6-1; Elena Vasquez dissented on concentration grounds.
